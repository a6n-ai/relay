---
title: Mailboxes
description: Invite a teammate with their own mailbox on your domain, and how they sign in.
---

A mailbox account gives a teammate a personal address on one of your verified domains, such as `ravi@yourcompany.com`. It works as their sign-in and as their own inbox.

## Invite someone with a mailbox

1. Open Settings, then Invites, and choose Invite member.
2. Keep "With mailbox" selected. It is the default when you have a verified domain.
3. Enter their name and their personal email. Relay suggests a mailbox from the name; change it if you like.
4. Pick the app if you have more than one, and a role. Then send the invite.

The invite goes to their personal email. Choose "Without mailbox" to invite someone with only their own address.

## How sign-in works

The teammate accepts the invite first. Only then can they set a password, in Settings. Setting a password sooner would send sign-in codes to the mailbox instead of the personal email.

On the sign-in page they enter the mailbox address. Relay says "Check your email for a code" for every address. For a mailbox account the code goes to the recovery email, which is the personal email from the invite. After they set a password, they can sign in with the mailbox and password.

## What a personal mailbox can do

- Receive mail and send from the mailbox.
- A personal mailbox cannot send campaigns or API mail. Those use shared sender addresses.
- Owners and admins can read personal mailboxes unless the platform admin turns that off for your workspace.
